# Greenhouse Rebuild

The old greenhouse frame is sound but every pane needs replacing before winter.

## Glazing delivery

The replacement glazing panes arrive on Monday the fourteenth of March.
Bramwell Glassworks will unload them at the side gate, not the front drive.

## Frame repairs

Two of the cedar uprights are soft at the base and will be sistered rather than
replaced. The ironmongery is reusable once the old putty is chipped away.

## Budget

The glazing quote came to four hundred and twenty pounds including delivery.
